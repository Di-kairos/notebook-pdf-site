Privacy Policy

Notebook PDF · Effective November 8, 2026

Notebook PDF doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party services. The app makes no network requests.

Your files

Notebook PDF opens only the notebooks you choose and saves PDFs only where you choose. It converts them on your Mac; nothing from your files leaves your device. It doesn't run the code in your notebooks.

What stays on your device

Your layout choices — Show Code, Title Page, and paper size — are saved on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
